What My Channel Is Not

Important Expectations

My content is built for long-term discovery, not immediate viral reach.

Because the FornaTRON channel primarily focuses on "How To" content, my videos tend to gain views over time as people discover a product or service and search for help setting it up or using it.

Please do not depend on a FornaTRON video to generate a massive number of views immediately after publication. My content is designed to remain useful and discoverable over time.

I am not here to sell your product.

My goal is to educate my audience and give my genuine opinion about a product's functionality, features, and potential use cases.

I am not interested in affiliate commissions, promotional codes, commission-based sales arrangements, or having the content's success measured by the number of products sold.

I only feature products and services that make sense for my audience.

My audience primarily consists of gamers, streamers, and content creators looking for technology, products, and services that can improve their gaming, streaming, or content-creation experience.

This commonly includes creators producing content for platforms such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok, and similar platforms.

Because of this, I only consider products and services that I believe could genuinely provide value to the people watching my videos.
